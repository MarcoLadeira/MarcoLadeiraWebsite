Overview

Fenergo builds software for financial institutions to onboard clients and meet ongoing know-your-customer and anti-money-laundering obligations. I joined the core platform team as a graduate and have since worked across engineering teams and different areas of the product.

The problem

Client lifecycle workflows span services, data and user-facing steps. Engineers need to understand how a change behaves beyond the screen or service where it started.

Why it mattered to me

I wanted to understand the whole path from a business requirement to a change that people could use in production.

My role

  • On the core platform, I worked on event-driven back-end services, including patterns for keeping processing consistent across retries and concurrent updates.
  • In full-stack work, I contribute to requirements, design, implementation, debugging, testing and release validation with product and engineering colleagues.
  • As part of the company’s first AI initiative, I helped a small engineering team bring an application to beta and worked on language-model integrations for a regulated workflow.

How I work in this domain

Event handling
I work with patterns such as CQRS, idempotent event processing and optimistic concurrency where the system calls for them.
AI workflows
I account for review and error handling where a language model is part of a larger workflow.

Lessons

  1. Working across teams has made me more deliberate about what a system promises at its boundaries and where another team needs context.
  2. Understanding how a change will be tested and released makes the implementation easier to reason about.

Project status

I joined as a Graduate Software Engineer in September 2025 and was promoted to Associate Software Engineer in 2026.
