# Portfolio design system

The homepage makes the full name the headline. Its opening gives the visitor Marco’s current role, engineering scope, employer and two actions. A green Fenergo section establishes career progression before the independent projects. Each project has its own image treatment drawn from the real interface.

## Creative directions considered

| Direction | Composition | Trade-off |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Oversized name and product chapters | A Swiss-style wordmark, clear first-screen facts and large product frames | Separates the professional and independent work while leaving room for distinct project imagery. Chosen. |
| Portrait-led editorial | Lead with a portrait and a short account of Marco’s work beside it | Puts the person first, but asks a single photograph to carry both professional credibility and product range. |
| Screenshot contact sheet | A compact archive of product images and short labels | Makes browsing fast but gives technical and mobile detail too little space. |

Reference principles came from the type scale and economy of [Vercel Geist](https://vercel.com/font), the visual prioritisation in [Linear’s design refresh](https://linear.app/now/behind-the-latest-design-refresh), and the sequencing and pacing of [Framer award-winning editorial and portfolio work](https://www.framer.com/awards/). The design is original and uses Marco’s own project imagery.

## Implementation

- Geist is the self-hosted display face, with IBM Plex Sans for reading and Newsreader retained where long-form copy benefits from it. The local Geist font includes its OFL text.
- The main palette is near-white or charcoal with a green focus and accent colour. Vesta uses a dark blue surround, Oddlings lilac and SaveThePlanet deep green. Project interfaces preserve their original colours.
- The masthead uses the full uppercase name with Work, Experience, About and CV. Contact, Writing and the Lab remain in the footer. The favicon is a simple line.
- Motion is brief and optional. Essential content stays visible while reveals run. Long contents jumps are direct and project video waits for a visitor action.
- On smaller screens, content stacks in reading order. Case studies provide an expandable contents list and desktop keeps its persistent contents navigation.
