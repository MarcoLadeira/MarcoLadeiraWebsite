About

I’m Marco, a software engineer based in Dublin. I studied at Dundalk Institute of Technology, where I built software with other students and represented my final-year class.

From college to Fenergo

I worked in hospitality during my first years at college, then took on freelance development in my final year. Seeing our ORO team demonstrate a working pill dispenser at the Computing Expo was a highlight of the degree.

I joined Fenergo in September 2025. I started on the client lifecycle platform and have since worked with different teams across back-end services, full-stack development and AI.

What I’m building

Outside work I make Vesta, a workspace for AI-assisted coding, and ONE Oddlings, a puzzle game. They let me work on things I don’t get to do every day, from designing how a coding tool reports its progress to getting a game to feel right on a phone.
